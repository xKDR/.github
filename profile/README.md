# Hi there, welcome to XKDR Forum! 👋

### 🙋‍♀️ About XKDR
**XKDR Forum** is an inter-disciplinary, open-access research organization. We operate at the intersection of **economics, law, public administration, and data science** to reimagine pathways in public policy. 

Our mission is to build foundational knowledge, open datasets, and open-source analytical tools that help solve complex structural, legal, and economic challenges—primarily focusing on the Indian economy and public policy landscape.

---

### 🌈 How to Get Involved
We welcome researchers, data scientists, policy enthusiasts, and software engineers to collaborate! 

* **Open Data & Legal Tech:** Help us build tools for court data analysis, legal systems reform, and empirical research.
* **Economic & Policy Analytics:** Contribute to open models, statistical tools, and data imputation pipelines.
* **Join the Community:** Attend our monthly seminars (like our series on *Contracts and Contract Performance*) or engage with our published research.
* **Contribute Code:** Check out our open-source repositories, tackle open issues, or submit Pull Requests for data pipelines and research tooling.
